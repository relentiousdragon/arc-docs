I like baguette

## Reasons to like baguette

### 1. Tasty Ends
No baguette has ever made it home with both ends intact. Ripping off the crunchy tip (*le quignon*) on the street is an involuntary human reflex.

### 2. Tactical Portability
It naturally wedges under your armpit like an architectural blueprint. Hands-free carbohydrate transport. Peak ergonomics.

### 3. Natural Evolution into a Weapon
* **Hour 1:** Soft, warm, pure joy.
* **Hour 12:** Good sandwich.
* **Day 2:** Tent stake.
* **Day 3:** Concrete baseball bat. Viable for medieval combat.

### 4. Legally Protected by the Government
Not a joke: the **1993 Bread Decree** made it an actual law that traditional baguettes can only have 4 ingredients (flour, water, salt, yeast).
